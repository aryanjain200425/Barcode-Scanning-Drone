Teensy Quad — Live PID Tuning

Tune gains over USB, or wirelessly through the Pi's bridge. Attitude, motor pulse widths and link health are live either way.

Before you connect: follow the bench checklist in README.md (steps 1–6, props off) at least once on this firmware. Kd is tuned first, quad still, gains at zero P/I. Keep a hand near Space / KILL at all times. This page becomes the only flight-control input while it's connected — closing the tab, losing focus, or losing the link all trigger disarm within a fraction of a second (client-side, plus the bridge's own kill-on-disconnect, plus the firmware's 400 ms link-timeout failsafe). Over Wi-Fi there is a network between you and the aircraft: expect the failsafe to fire on congestion, and read "Tuning over Wi-Fi" in README.md first.
